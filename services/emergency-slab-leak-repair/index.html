Most slab leaks are found during the day and scheduled for repair within days. An emergency is different: water is actively entering your living space, floors are buckling, or a pipe has burst under the slab and you cannot stop it. In those moments, every hour of standing water means more damage to flooring, drywall, cabinets, and the slab itself.

Our emergency service is built for exactly this. We answer around the clock, talk you through shutting off the water, and dispatch for same-day response across Lutz and the surrounding communities. The first visit stops the damage; the permanent repair follows once the leak is located.

What Counts as an Emergency

Standing water on floors with no visible source is the clearest sign of a burst line under the slab. So is a sudden loss of water pressure combined with the sound of rushing water, or a water heater that runs constantly because it is feeding a break. If drywall is wicking water upward or floors are warm and wet, treat it as urgent.

A high water bill or a faint warm spot, with no active flooding, is not an emergency, but it still deserves prompt detection. Call us during business hours for those and we will schedule you quickly. When water is moving, call now regardless of the hour.

  • Standing water with no visible source
  • Sudden pressure loss plus rushing water sounds
  • Water heater running nonstop
  • Wet, buckling floors or wicking drywall
  • Any active flooding you cannot stop

What to Do Before We Arrive

First, shut off your main water valve. It is usually near the street by your meter or where the line enters the house. Turning it clockwise stops the flow and stops the damage from growing. If you cannot find it, call us and we will walk you through it on the phone.

Next, protect what you can: move rugs and furniture off wet areas, and do not use electrical appliances sitting in water. Take photos of everything for your insurance claim. Then leave the cleanup to us. Do not try to break concrete yourself; you will only make the repair harder.

  • Shut off the main water valve to stop the flow
  • Move rugs, furniture, and electronics off wet floors
  • Photograph all damage for your insurance claim
  • Avoid electrical items in standing water
  • Do not break concrete yourself
