5 Warning Signs of a Slab Leak

Slab leaks rarely announce themselves loudly. Most Lutz homeowners notice something small first, then realize the water bill has been climbing for months. Here is what to watch for:

  • A warm spot on the floor. A hot water line leaking under the slab heats the concrete above it. If one patch of tile or wood feels warm, that is the classic sign.
  • The sound of running water. Hear water moving when every tap is off? That is pressurized water escaping under your foundation.
  • A water bill that jumped. Compare this month to the same month last year. A sudden rise with no change in habits points underground.
  • Damp carpet or a musty smell. Moisture wicking up through the slab feeds mold in flooring and baseboards.
  • Low water pressure. A significant leak bleeds off pressure before water reaches your fixtures.

One sign is enough to call. Detection takes under two hours and tells you exactly where you stand.

Why Lutz Homes Get Slab Leaks

Most Lutz homes sit on slab-on-grade foundations poured over copper or galvanized water lines. Decades of sandy soil shifting, hard Central Florida water, and warm ground temperatures wear those lines from the outside in. Homes built from the 1970s through the early 2000s are in the prime window for pinhole leaks and fitting failures.

The leak itself is only half the problem. Pressurized water erodes the soil supporting your slab, which can lead to settling and cracking if it runs for months. That is why fast detection matters more than anything: find it early and the fix is a fitting, find it late and the fix is a foundation conversation.

Slab Leak Questions, Answered

Straight answers before you call.

How do you find a leak under concrete without tearing up my floors?

We use acoustic listening equipment and thermal imaging cameras to pinpoint the leak location, often within inches, before any concrete is opened.

Do you have to jackhammer my slab?

Not always. Many leaks can be fixed by rerouting the line through walls or attic space, which avoids breaking concrete entirely.

How long does a slab leak repair take?

Most detection visits take under two hours. Repairs range from half a day for a reroute to two days for under-slab work.

Will my homeowner's insurance cover it?

Many policies cover the resulting water damage and sometimes the access needed to reach the pipe. We document everything for your claim.

What are the first signs of a slab leak?

A hot spot on the floor, the sound of running water when nothing is on, damp carpet edges, or a water bill that jumped for no reason.
