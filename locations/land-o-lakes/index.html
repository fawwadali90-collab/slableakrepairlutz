Frequently Asked Questions

About slab leak work in Land O'Lakes.

Does well water cause more slab leaks?

It can. Well water chemistry varies, and aggressive water pits copper faster. If your fixtures stain heavily, have your lines checked.

Do you serve the Connerton and Asbel Estates areas?

Yes, we cover all of Land O'Lakes including Connerton, Asbel Estates, and the US-41 corridor.

My home has a septic system. Does that matter?

Not for the supply lines under your slab. Septic affects drainage, while slab leaks are pressurized supply lines.

What ZIP codes do you cover in Land O'Lakes?

We cover 34637, 34638, and 34639.

How do I know if my leak is under the slab?

Warm floor spots, running water sounds with fixtures off, and rising water bills are the classic signs. Our detection visit confirms it.
