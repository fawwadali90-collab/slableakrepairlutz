Why Lutz Homes Get Slab Leaks

Most Lutz homes sit on slab-on-grade foundations poured over copper or galvanized lines. Decades of soil shifting, hard water, and warm ground temperatures wear those lines from the outside in.

A hot spot on the floor, the sound of running water when nothing is on, or a water bill that jumped for no reason are the classic signs. The longer it runs, the more it erodes the soil under your slab.

Slab Leak Questions, Answered

Straight answers before you call.

How do you find a leak under concrete without tearing up my floors?

We use acoustic listening equipment and thermal imaging cameras to pinpoint the leak location, often within inches, before any concrete is opened.

Do you have to jackhammer my slab?

Not always. Many leaks can be fixed by rerouting the line through walls or attic space, which avoids breaking concrete entirely.

How long does a slab leak repair take?

Most detection visits take under two hours. Repairs range from half a day for a reroute to two days for under-slab work.

Will my homeowner's insurance cover it?

Many policies cover the resulting water damage and sometimes the access needed to reach the pipe. We document everything for your claim.

What are the first signs of a slab leak?

A hot spot on the floor, the sound of running water when nothing is on, damp carpet edges, or a water bill that jumped for no reason.

Do you serve areas outside Lutz?

Yes. We cover Tampa, Wesley Chapel, Land O'Lakes, Odessa, Cheval, and surrounding Hillsborough and Pasco communities.
